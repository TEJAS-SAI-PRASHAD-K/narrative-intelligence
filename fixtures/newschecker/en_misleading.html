Claim

The opposition INDIA bloc organised a massive rally at Delhi’s Ramlila Maidan on Sunday, November 1, demanding the resignation of CEC Gyanesh Kumar and protesting against alleged electoral irregularities. 

Following the rally, a video of a man criticising Congress leader Rahul Gandhi and saying that he had only come to visit Delhi has gone viral on social media, with users claiming that it shows a participant at the November 1 rally who was not there to listen to support the cause, but had merely come to explore the city.

Also Read: Video Of Argentina’s 2022 World Cup Celebrations Falsely Shared As INDIA Bloc’s Ramlila Maidan Protest

Fact

A reverse image search led us to an X post by @AshutoshkrUp, dated December 15, 2019, carrying the same footage. We also found the clip uploaded on YouTube by @WoldsVerticalVideos7773 in December 2019, confirming that the footage predates the November 1 INDIA bloc rally in Delhi. 

RESULT
Misleading
